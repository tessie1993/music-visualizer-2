# MusicViz Product Review — fresh-eyes critique (2026-08-10)

Six independent reviewers reconstructed the user experience purely from source, resources
and manifest (no docs), judged against Poweramp, Musicolet, projectM, Wallpaper Engine and
Apple Music. Every claim carries file:line evidence in the underlying review data
(workflow wf_2366e47d-bbb). This document is the distilled canonical record.


## The listening experience (Player, Library, search, queue, playlists, favourites, history, lyrics, EQ, metadata/artwork)

MusicViz opens directly onto a Player tab (there is no dashboard Home): a hero card with large artwork, favourite heart, scene chip, a transport card with a waveform seek bar, shuffle/repeat, A-B loop, a live 24-bar spectrum, an up-next preview, and quick actions (mic input, capture other apps, sleep 30m, shuffle-all). A Library tab browses device music via MediaStore in five tabs (Tracks/Albums/Artists/Playlists/Folders) with in-context permission prompting, SAF folder imports, per-track menus (play next, queue, playlist, edit tags app-side), and long-press drag playlist reordering. Tapping any track queues the whole list being viewed (windowed at 1001 items) so Next/Previous behave like a real player; playback runs on a single shared ExoPlayer published through a Media3 MediaSession foreground service, so music survives backgrounding, drives the lock screen, and resumes after process death via a media-button receiver. A fullscreen Now Playing overlay layers transport, a queue panel, and synced .lrc lyrics over the live GL visualizer. Settings hold speed/pitch, skip-silence, fade-on-pause/skip, sleep timer with finish-track mode, and a device Equalizer/BassBoost/Loudness chain. Favourites, play history and listening-time are recorded to disk — but almost none of that record is ever shown back to the user.

**Strengths**

- Single shared ExoPlayer with reference-counted lifetime, MediaSession service, and correct audio-focus policy — playback genuinely survives the screen, transport controls always drive the audible player, and calls/nav prompts duck instead of killing music _(PlaybackEngine.kt:76-92 (handleAudioFocus rationale), PlaybackEngine.kt:162-240 (UI+service holds), PlaybackService.kt:4)_
- Tapping a track queues the list you were looking at (album, folder, search results), windowed at 1001 items with the window slid to keep it full at edges; Next/Previous wrap, and Previous uses the 3-second restart convention — the transport behaves like Poweramp's, and the defect it fixes (one-item queues) is documented _(PlaybackQueue.kt:13-86 (window/contextFor/PREV_RESTART_MS), PlayerViewModel.kt:2556-2589 (playFrom funnel), PlayerViewMo)_
- The sleep timer is engineered for its real use case: it lives with the player (not the screen), so it survives swipe-away; finish-track mode waits for the item transition rather than isPlaying (correct under repeat-one and auto-advance); the fade is skipped in finish-track mode to avoid a volume pop; volume is always restored _(SleepTimer.kt:16-176 (full mechanism incl. awaitCurrentTrackDone), PlayerViewModel.kt:1359-1416)_
- System media-resumption is actually wired end-to-end: the MediaButtonReceiver is declared with a documented rationale for why Media3 needs it, and onPlaybackResumption serves the last-played track so the app reappears in the System UI media carousel after process death _(AndroidManifest.xml:124-146, PlaybackService.kt:62-80 and 133-147)_
- The seek bar is the track's own loudness waveform (reduced from analysis data the app already computes), with A-B loop region tinting, drag-commits-on-release to avoid decoder thrash, a visible playhead, and a plain-bar fallback for unanalysed tracks _(PlayerPanels.kt:52-153 (WaveformSeekBar), PlayerViewModel.kt:2213-2239 (peak-per-bucket waveformOf))_
- Synced lyrics done properly for local files: sidecar .lrc preferred over embedded (for both file:// and SAF document trees), multi-timestamp chorus lines expanded, hundredths-vs-thousandths fraction widths handled, tap-a-line-to-seek, and auto-scroll that yields while the user reads back and resumes after 5s idle _(Lyrics.kt:62-174 (LyricsLoader/parse), PlayerPanels.kt:155-287 (LyricsPanel, rememberFollowsPlayback))_
- Unusual data durability for a music app: every store (history, library, playlists) writes via atomic rename+fsync, quarantines corrupt files instead of silently starting fresh, and refuses to write over files it could not read — explicitly preventing the 'one bad read erases your whole listening record' failure _(HistoryStore.kt:81-109 (three-outcome readLocked), TrackLibrary.kt:141-175 (mutate/readLocked/writeLocked), MusicPlaylis)_
- Listening time is measured honestly: tick-based accrual so seeks don't count as listening, a 5s cap so process suspension isn't booked, flush on track change/pause/teardown, and monotonic recently-played timestamps so rapid skips keep user-created order _(PlayerViewModel.kt:2404-2439 (accrueListenTime), PlayerViewModel.kt:4011-4017 (MAX_LISTEN_TICK_MS), HistoryStore.kt:145-)_
- Playlist UX above indie-app grade: long-press drag reorder with the up/down buttons deliberately kept for switch/screen-reader users, save-queue-as-playlist through the same validated naming dialog as create/rename, duplicate-add as documented no-op, rename that surfaces failure instead of silently closing _(LibraryScreen.kt:513-618 (PlaylistTracks drag + rationale), PlayerPanels.kt:289-473 (QueuePanel save, playlistNameAccept)_
- The equalizer card distinguishes three states most apps conflate — 'play something first' (no audio session yet), 'device refused the effect', and per-effect grants (BassBoost without Equalizer) — and every audiofx call is defensive with settings persisted and reattached on session change _(EqualizerSettings.kt:25-36 and 79-103, AudioFxController.kt:88-140 and 262-282, PlayerViewModel.kt:3910-3915 (onAudioSes)_
- Cross-provider dedup by display-name+size so adding /Music as a SAF folder on a device MediaStore already indexed does not double-list every track; pre-v3 entries backfill and collapse without losing cached analysis _(TrackLibrary.kt:188-247 (identityKey/mergeAdds/richer))_
- Artwork pipeline is production-grade: decode off main thread, downsampled to 384px, misses cached so art-less tracks never re-open the retriever, and a deterministic per-uri gradient placeholder so tracks without art still look like themselves everywhere _(Artwork.kt:37-95 (ArtworkCache), Artwork.kt:182-241 (TrackArtwork/placeholderBrush))_
- Empty states teach instead of dead-ending: the no-lyrics panel explains the .lrc convention and the no-network reality, the empty queue explains that playing from a list queues the list, and the fresh-install hero disables Resume until history exists (fixing a documented dead button) _(PlayerPanels.kt:169-183 and 315-325, PlayerScreen.kt:167-180 and 289-296)_
- Search is debounced (250ms), multi-term AND across title/artist/album/folder/genre, dedupes device-vs-imported rows preferring the device index, and plays the result list as the queue so Next walks your search hits; playlists and visual presets are searched in the same overlay _(AppShell.kt:387-564 (SearchScreen), SearchMatcher.kt:8-45)_
- Repeat-one edge cases handled where most apps break: sleep finish-track detects the repeat-one restart via both transition and discontinuity events idempotently; queue row keys are occurrence-based so removing row 0 doesn't rekey every row _(SleepTimer.kt:133-175, PlayerPanels.kt:411-428 (queueRowKeys))_

**Weaknesses**

- **HIGH** — The queue and playback position do not survive process death or reboot. Resumption (system carousel or in-app Resume) rebuilds exactly ONE track starting at 0:00; auto-resume is off by default and also prepares only the last single track at 0:00. Poweramp and Musicolet both restore the full queue at the exact position.
  - User impact: A daily listener 40 minutes into a DJ mix or 30 tracks into a queued album loses everything whenever Android reclaims the process overnight — the most common resume scenario for a phone music player.
  - Evidence: PlaybackService.kt:121-131 ('positions are not persisted anywhere in the app... Starts from 0:00', single-item MediaItemsWithStartPosition at :146), PlayerViewM
- **HIGH** — There is no playback error handling anywhere: no Player.Listener implements onPlayerError, and no UI surface reports a failed track. A deleted file, moved SD card, or revoked SAF permission mid-queue silently stops playback with no toast, no skip-to-next, no explanation.
  - User impact: Users with stale library entries (SAF uris go stale routinely) tap a row and nothing audible happens; playback dies mid-queue at night with no recovery — top-tier players toast the failure and auto-skip.
  - Evidence: grep 'onPlayerError|PlaybackException' across app/src/main returns zero hits; the only listener is PlayerViewModel.kt:3856-3943 which handles transitions, seeks
- **HIGH** — Favourites are write-only. Hearts exist on the Player hero, Now Playing, and as a star glyph in queue rows, but no screen lists favourites, no library filter exists (tabs are Tracks/Albums/Artists/Playlists/Folders), and there is no way to play them. FavouritesStore's own doc promises 'a shelf on Home and a filter in the library' — neither exists since the Home dashboard was removed.
  - User impact: A listener who hearts tracks for weeks discovers the collection is unreachable — the core loop of favouriting (mark now, play later) is half-implemented.
  - Evidence: FavouritesStore.kt:6-8 (promised surfaces), LibraryScreen.kt:92 (tab list has no Favourites), PlayerScreen.kt:55-57 ('The old dashboard Home... is gone'); grep 
- **HIGH** — Listening history and stats are collected meticulously but never shown: HistoryStore.mostPlayed() is dead code (zero callers), recentlyPlayed() only gates the Resume/Shuffle buttons, and per-track listenedMs is accumulated to disk but rendered nowhere. There is no Recently Played list, no Most Played, no stats screen.
  - User impact: The app pays the full engineering and disk cost of a listening-history feature and delivers none of its user value — Musicolet/Poweramp users expect at minimum a recently-played list to re-find yesterday's track.
  - Evidence: HistoryStore.kt:202-211 (recentlyPlayed/mostPlayed), grep shows mostPlayed has no callers and recentlyPlayed only used at PlayerScreen.kt:86 and PlayerViewModel
- **HIGH** — No album art on the lock screen or media notification: MediaItems are built with title/artist metadata only — artworkData/artworkUri are never set anywhere — even though the app already decodes embedded art for its own UI via ArtworkCache.
  - User impact: Every glance at the lock screen, notification shade, or a paired watch shows a generic icon where every commercial player shows the sleeve; the app looks broken/unfinished at the OS surface users see most.
  - Evidence: PlayerViewModel.kt:2636-2669 (both mediaItemFor overloads set only setTitle/setArtist), grep 'setArtwork|artworkData|artworkUri' across app/src/main returns zer
- **MEDIUM** — 5,000-track ergonomics are missing: the Tracks tab is a single flat list sorted by title only (no sort options), with no fast-scroll/alphabet scrubber, no durations shown on rows, and search caps at 30 track results.
  - User impact: Reaching artists starting with 'S' in a 5,000-track library means minutes of flinging; finding 'recently added' music is impossible (DATE_ADDED is queried into DeviceTrack.addedSec and then never used anywhere).
  - Evidence: PlayerViewModel.kt:1810 (single COLLATE NOCASE title sort), grep 'fastscroll|scrollbar' zero UI hits, SearchMatcher.kt:10 (TRACK_LIMIT=30), PlayerViewModel.kt:1
- **MEDIUM** — 'Shuffle all' does not shuffle the library — it shuffles listening history (at most 100 recently-played entries), and there is no play-all/shuffle affordance on the Tracks tab at all; group drill-ins have the buttons but the top-level library does not. Shuffle can also never cover more than the 1001-track queue window.
  - User impact: The most common 'just play my music' gesture plays a loop of what you already listened to; a new install with a full library has no one-tap way to shuffle everything.
  - Evidence: PlayerViewModel.kt:2485-2489 (shuffleAllHistory uses recentlyPlayed(100)), LibraryScreen.kt:120-132 (TrackList has no header actions; GroupList:331-336 does), P
- **MEDIUM** — Queue reordering is one-step 'move up' taps only (no move-down, no drag), even though moveQueueItem(from,to) supports arbitrary moves and the playlist editor already implements long-press drag.
  - User impact: Pulling track 20 to play next is 19 taps; the asymmetry with the playlist editor (which got the drag gesture precisely because '49 taps of the up arrow' was recognized as bad) makes the queue feel unfinished.
  - Evidence: PlayerPanels.kt:383-387 (only KeyboardArrowUp), VisualizerScreen.kt:260 (onMoveUp = move(it, it-1)), PlayerViewModel.kt:2323-2331 (general moveQueueItem exists)
- **MEDIUM** — Play counts inflate: recordPlay fires on every EVENT_MEDIA_ITEM_TRANSITION with no reason filter and no minimum-listen threshold — repeat-one restarts and instant skips each book a full play and bump recently-played.
  - User impact: Leaving one song on repeat overnight books hundreds of 'plays'; skimming a queue pollutes 'most played' and history-derived features (resume, shuffle-history) with tracks the user rejected — listenedMs tie-breaking only partially compensates.
  - Evidence: PlayerViewModel.kt:3863-3884 (recordPlay inside onEvents on any transition), HistoryStore.kt:205-211 (playCount primary sort)
- **MEDIUM** — The A-B loop is enforced from the 500ms poll, so playback overshoots point B by up to half a second on every pass, and neither endpoint can be adjusted after being set (any transport interaction just clears it).
  - User impact: For the musicians/language learners the feature targets, a loop that audibly bleeds past B each cycle and can't be fine-tuned feels broken compared to dedicated loopers.
  - Evidence: PlayerViewModel.kt:2284-2289 (enforceAbLoop 'Called from the poll'), PlayerViewModel.kt:3994-4007 (500ms loop), PlayerViewModel.kt:2268-2278 (cycle-only API)
- **MEDIUM** — Embedded lyrics are read from METADATA_KEY_WRITER — the songwriter credit tag — not from USLT/SYLT lyric frames (which MediaMetadataRetriever cannot expose). Most files with real embedded lyrics show 'No lyrics', and a file with a writer credit can display the composer's name as its lyric sheet.
  - User impact: The advertised 'words written into the file's own tags are read too' rarely works, and when it does it can show wrong content; only sidecar .lrc users get the real feature.
  - Evidence: Lyrics.kt:114-119 (extractMetadata(METADATA_KEY_WRITER)), PlayerPanels.kt:176-178 (UI promise)
- **MEDIUM** — Albums merge by name alone across artists, and the Albums/Artists lists are text-only rows (no artwork grid, no artist attribution, no year) sorted alphabetically.
  - User impact: Two different artists' 'Greatest Hits' become one merged album; browsing by album — a primarily visual activity in every commercial player — is a wall of text.
  - Evidence: LibraryScreen.kt:112 (groupBy { it.album }), LibraryScreen.kt:344-357 (text-only GroupList rows)
- **LOW** — seekBy(±ms) is dead code — no skip-forward/back buttons exist anywhere — and formatClock renders hour-long tracks as '75:00' instead of '1:15:00'.
  - User impact: Long-form listeners (mixes, audiobooks) have only the seek bar for navigation, where a 1px drag on a 2-hour file jumps ~30 seconds; timestamps read wrong past an hour.
  - Evidence: PlayerViewModel.kt:2524-2528 (seekBy, grep shows no callers), PlayerScreen.kt:746-749 and VisualizerScreen.kt:462-465 (mm:ss only)
- **LOW** — Sleep timer offers only fixed 15/30/45/60 choices buried in Settings › Audio; the Player quick action hardcodes 30 minutes with no way to pick a duration at the point of use.
  - User impact: The bedtime flow — the one moment the timer is used — requires either accepting 30m or a trip into Settings; no custom duration, no end-of-queue option.
  - Evidence: PlaybackSettings.kt:23 (SLEEP_TIMER_CHOICES), PlayerScreen.kt:689-696 (hardcoded startSleepTimer(30))
- **LOW** — The mini-player shows a music-note glyph instead of the track's artwork and its progress line is not seekable; the manifest's claim that the session reaches 'watches, Android Auto' overstates reality — there is no MediaLibraryService/browse tree, so Android Auto users get no browsing.
  - User impact: The always-visible mini-player looks generic next to Poweramp's; in-car users can control the current queue but cannot start anything.
  - Evidence: AppShell.kt:316-323 (Icons.Filled.MusicNote), AndroidManifest.xml:110-113 (Auto claim), grep 'MediaLibraryService|onGetLibraryRoot' zero hits

**Missing table stakes**

- Persistent queue + exact playback position restore across process death and reboot (the defining baseline of Poweramp/Musicolet)
- Album artwork on the lock screen / media notification / watch (artworkData on MediaItems)
- A browsable Favourites list and Recently Played / Most Played views — the data already exists on disk
- Library sort options (artist, album, date added, duration) and an alphabet fast-scroll scrubber for large libraries
- One-tap shuffle of the entire library (not just listening history)
- Playback-error feedback with auto-skip past unplayable/moved files
- True crossfade between tracks and ReplayGain/volume levelling (the app is explicit that fade-on-edge is not crossfade)
- Genre browsing tab (genre is already extracted and stored per track)
- M3U/PLS playlist import & export for users migrating from other players
- Home-screen widget with transport controls
- Go-to-album / go-to-artist navigation from the now-playing track
- Per-track resume bookmarks for long files (mixes, audiobooks)
- Skip-forward/back ±10s buttons for long-form content (the ViewModel API exists, unused)

**Wow opportunities**

- Full session restore: persist queue + index + position on the existing 500ms poll (the atomic-write infrastructure in data/ is already built) and feed it to both auto-resume and onPlaybackResumption — reopening mid-track with your whole queue intact would instantly match Poweramp on its signature strength.
- A 'Listening' screen from data already on disk: Most Played, Recently Played, total listen-time per track/artist, plus the missing Favourites shelf — HistoryStore.playCount and listenedMs are collected and never shown; this is pure UI over paid-for plumbing and would out-feature Musicolet's stats.
- Attach the already-decoded embedded artwork (ArtworkCache) as artworkData on MediaItems: a small change that transforms the lock screen, notification, and Bluetooth displays from generic to premium.
- Beat-snapped A-B looping: the app already knows each analysed track's BPM and beat grid — quantize A/B points to the nearest bar and enforce the loop with a positioned PlayerMessage instead of the 500ms poll. Sample-accurate, musically-aligned looping is something no mainstream mobile player offers.
- Key/BPM-aware library superpowers: analysis already yields musical key and BPM per track (shown as chips). Add BPM/key sorting and a Camelot-wheel 'plays well next' suggestion when building queues — a DJ-adjacent feature that turns the analysis engine into a listening differentiator, not just a visuals input.
- Render the synced lyric line inside the visualizer canvas (the GL renderer and timed lyrics both exist): karaoke-grade now-playing and shareable lyric-video exports through the existing export pipeline would be a genuine 'nobody else does this' moment for content creators.

## The visual experience: scenes, customization, presets, palettes, modulation, transitions, touch, safety, MilkDrop, performance scaling

MusicViz's visual core is a GL ES 3.0 multi-scene renderer offering roughly 58 selectable styles across seven genuinely different rendering technologies: 9 CPU particle scenes, 22 single-pass fragment-shader scenes, a full Navier-Stokes fluid family (Fluid / Curl Flow / Water with a liquid-ink film), a Cymatics standing-wave family with 10 authored substyles (Chladni Sand, Drumhead, Faraday, etc.), a raymarched 3D-fractal "Hyperspace" family with 10 substyles (Polytope, Caduceus, Resonant Wormhole, etc.) coupled to an underlying fluid "melt", an oscilloscope Beam scene, and real MilkDrop support via an embedded libprojectM v4.1.7. Users customize through a Visuals hub with Presets / Styles / Customize / Textures / Takes tabs; the Customize panel exposes ~140 parameters across Motion, Shape, Behavior, Color, FX, Fluid, and style-specific Cymatics/Hyperspace tabs, plus a live GLSL editor for shader scenes. On top sit three chainable LFOs, two beat-triggered ADSR envelopes, per-tab randomization with per-control locks, 21 procedural palettes plus 5 Crameri perceptually-uniform cyclic colour maps and a custom palette maker, 128 scene transitions (5 built-in + 123 vendored gl-transitions), one-finger fluid "smear" and pinch/twist canvas gestures, preset save/share (link or file), a visual playlist and beat-aware random mode, performance "takes" recording, and a WCAG-grounded photosensitivity safety system.

**Strengths**

- Exceptional breadth AND depth of scene families — this is not one engine with 58 skins but seven real technologies: distance-estimated raymarching with per-substyle Lipschitz correction and bounding-sphere culling, a full GPU fluid solver with bloom/sunrays/particle passes, Bessel/Chladni cymatics, projectM, particles, an oscilloscope beam, and shader scenes _(SceneIds.kt:4-43 (38 base ids); VisualStyleCatalog.kt:100-482 (10+10 authored substyles with per-style lipschitz, tint, )_
- A universal customization contract competitors don't have: nearly every Customize parameter works on every style, enforced by a composite pass with per-texture gates so nothing is double-graded, and controls a style cannot read are hidden or given honest hints naming which styles ignore them _(composite_frag.glsl:45-115 (uGateA/uGateB per-texture gate design), VisualsHub.kt:836-935 (per-slice fluid/shape/color g)_
- Modular-synth-grade modulation: three chainable LFOs (5 waveforms, BPM sync with beat divisions 1/16..8, LFO1 can drive LFO2/3 rate/depth) plus two beat-triggered ADSR envelopes with velocity-scaled attacks, band-gated sustain with hysteresis, and multi-target routing — beyond anything in Poweramp, Musicolet, or the Apple Music visualizer _(Lfo.kt:24-72 (44 targets incl. chain targets), Lfo.kt:145-193 (chain evaluation), Adsr.kt:83-143 (beat-impulse-scaled at)_
- Serious palette discipline: append-only palette table to protect saved presets, dual-slot palette blending, Fabio Crameri cyclic perceptually-uniform LUTs for banding-free circular quantities, artwork-derived palettes, and blue-noise dithering as the final pass to kill OLED banding _(SceneParams.kt:371-402 (APPEND ONLY contract, 21 palettes), CyclicPalettes.kt:9-47 (bamO/brocO/corkO/romaO/vikO atlas, w)_
- 128 scene transitions done right: the vendored gl-transitions corpus is spliced into the composite at selection time (no 123-program startup cost), and both sides of every transition pass through the full FX chain with their own grade gates so effects never pop off mid-switch _(TransitionCatalog.kt:9-148 (corpus vendoring, lazy splice, per-frame lookup caching), composite_frag.glsl:330-363 (getFr)_
- The randomizer is a masterclass in respecting user work: scoped to the visible tab, per-control lock chips whose keys are test-enforced against drift, curated watchable ranges, rare effects kept rare, re-roll to prevent dead-feeling presses, and a NEVER_ROLLED list with stated reasons for every exclusion _(ParamRandomizer.kt:5-116 (tab scoping rationale, REROLLS, NEVER_ROLLED map), CustomizeTabs.kt:120-175 (LockChip with 48d)_
- Touch interaction is production-thought-out: one-finger smear routes into the water surface or shared ripple overlay (never applied twice), pinch/twist maps onto the real Zoom/Rotation params so gestures persist into presets, takes, and exports, with a single gesture detector to avoid pointer fights _(TouchTransform.kt:3-36 (gesture-to-slider mapping rationale, rotation-as-speed), VisualizerScreen.kt:100-135 (unified de)_
- Takes — recording a performance as parameter keyframes rather than pixels, replayable over the live canvas and re-renderable at any quality on export — is a genuinely novel content-creator feature no mainstream competitor ships _(VisualsHub.kt:1184-1287 (TakesTab: keyframe counts, replay progress, 'Render this take on the next export'), VisualsHub.)_
- MilkDrop integration is respectful of the format: Customize transforms work on .milk via a post pass, the palette only ever tints (exact no-op at 0 so presets keep their authored colours), texture import with preset reload, parse errors surfaced where the file was picked, and unavailability stated honestly rather than a black screen _(ProjectMScene.kt:221-236 (palette-tint-only rationale), ProjectMScene.kt:104-116 (driver-rejection degradation with mess)_
- Performance scaling is real engineering, not a checkbox: 5 fluid quality tiers spanning 16x particle count, a Welford rolling-FPS monitor that only downgrades on sustained deficit and never fights the user upward, lazy compilation of the 20 substyles to avoid ~130 startup shader compiles, cached uniform locations, and float-precision time wrapping for days-long wallpaper uptime _(FluidQuality.kt:19-42, PerformanceMonitor.kt:11-62, VisualStyleCatalog.kt:487-506 (lazyIds rationale), VisualizerRendere)_
- Visuals-nerd extension points nobody else ships on mobile: a live in-app GLSL fragment editor per shader scene (with error reporting and rotation-safe drafts) and user-injectable fluid force/dye shaders _(VisualsHub.kt:1424-1460 (GlslHubTab with uFlow documentation), VisualizerRenderer.kt:362-370 (submitFluidInjectionShader)_

## Creation and export (Export Studio, video export, takes, clip editing, share flows)

MusicViz's creation pipeline has three parts. First, an offline video exporter (opened from Settings › Export › "Export video…") that re-renders the current visual style frame-by-frame against a precomputed audio-analysis timeline into an H.264/AAC MP4 — 720p/1080p/4K, 30/60 fps, six aspect ratios (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 4:3, 21:9), always the full length of the current track, saved to Movies/MusicViz via MediaStore or to a user-picked folder, then shared through the system share sheet with no watermark. Second, "Takes": a record button in the Visuals hub captures the live performance as tiny JSON parameter automation (not pixels), which can be replayed over the live canvas or selected to drive an export so a phone-improvised set re-renders at 4K. Third, the "Studio" tab: a clip library scoped to Movies/MusicViz plus an editor that trims (6-frame filmstrip + range slider), grades (7 look presets + brightness/contrast/saturation/hue sliders), changes speed (0.25–4x), rotates, reframes with crop, mutes, and burns a caption, then re-encodes via Media3 Transformer (lossless container-rewrite on trim-only edits) to a new file. A creator's journey to a social post is long: render the whole song offline (minutes, app must stay in the foreground), find the clip in Studio, blind-trim it without any playback preview, render a second time, then hit the share sheet.

## Ambient and system integration

Outside its own screens, MusicViz offers: a live wallpaper that re-renders the exact style/parameters the app was last showing (including a loaded MilkDrop .milk and the photosensitivity limits), reacting to the app's music via an in-process AudioBus and drifting on gentle synthetic "idle features" the rest of the time; a "Visualize other apps" mode that captures Spotify/YouTube/game audio via Android 10 playback capture behind a mediaProjection foreground service with an ongoing notification, plus an optional notification-listener bridge that names what the other app is playing; a microphone "Live input" mode with room-tuning profiles; a Media3 MediaSessionService with a manifest MediaButtonReceiver so playback survives the screen, works from lock screen/Bluetooth, and resumes from System UI after process death; and an automatic second-screen mode that moves (not mirrors) the GL canvas to an HDMI/cast display while the phone becomes the control surface. ## competitive-position

MusicViz is a fully offline Android app that fuses three product categories into one shell: a complete local music player (Library with Tracks/Albums/Artists/Playlists/Folders tabs, queue, shuffle/repeat, speed/pitch, EQ, lyrics, sleep timer), a GPU visualizer with 38 native scene families plus an embedded libprojectM MilkDrop engine, and a creator studio that records VJ "takes" and re-renders them offline up to 4K60 with the audio muxed in. It also extends outward as a live wallpaper, a second-screen presentation output, and a "visualize other apps" mode that captures Spotify/YouTube audio via MediaProjection and names the track via a notification-listener MediaSession bridge. The app holds no INTERNET permission at all — preset sharing works by compressing the entire preset into a musicviz://preset deep link. The five-tab shell (Player / Library / Visuals / Studio / Settings) makes it read as a player first, but the depth is overwhelmingly on the visuals/creator side (~130+ customize parameters, 3 LFO slots, ADSR envelopes, 128 scene transitions, custom GLSL scenes).
