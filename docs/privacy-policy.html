Geode — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Geode is an offline music player and visualizer for Android. This policy describes what the app does with your information. The short version: Geode collects nothing, sends nothing and has no servers.

Information the app does not collect

Geode does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. Specifically, the app has:

  • no user accounts, sign-in or registration;
  • no analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs;
  • no advertising identifiers;
  • no INTERNET permission — the app is technically incapable of sending your data anywhere.

Information the app accesses on your device

To do its job Geode reads data that never leaves your device:

Your presets, playlists, track edits and settings To remember your setup between sessions The app's private storage on your device

Permissions

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (Android 13+) / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below): required to find and play the music already on your device. Nothing else is read.
  • RECORD_AUDIO (microphone): used by two optional features only — Live input and Visualize other apps (Android requires the microphone permission for its playback-capture API even though no microphone is involved). It is requested at the moment you switch one of those features on, never at launch. What is captured is analysed in memory to drive the visuals and is never written to disk or transmitted. If you never turn these features on, the microphone is never accessed.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): for the playback controls notification and the ongoing notifications Android requires while Visualize other apps or a video render is running.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE (with the media-playback, media-projection and media-processing types): lets playback, capture and video rendering keep running while the app is not on screen, always with a visible notification.
  • VIBRATE: haptic feedback for the app's controls.
  • WAKE_LOCK: keeps music playing reliably while the screen is off. It grants no access to any data.

Geode requests no camera, contacts, location or Internet permission.

Visualize other apps (playback capture)

If you turn this feature on, Android shows its own system consent dialog before any capture starts, and an ongoing notification is displayed the whole time the feature is active. Geode reads the device's audio output solely to animate the visuals: the samples pass through an in-memory analysis buffer and are never saved, recorded or sent anywhere. The capture stops when you switch the feature off, dismiss it from the notification, or the system ends it.

Now playing access (notification access)

Optionally, you can grant Geode notification access in system settings so it can show which app and track the device is playing (for example the title playing in another music app). Geode uses this access only to read the media sessions Android exposes through it; notification contents are not read, stored or transmitted. You can revoke this at any time in Settings → Notifications → Notification access, and the feature simply goes without labels.

Video export

When you export a visualization, Geode writes an MP4 file to the location you choose in Android's save dialog. The file stays on your device unless you choose to share it — at which point the app you pick (for example a messaging or cloud-storage app) handles it under its own privacy policy.

Backups

If Android backup is enabled on your device, your Geode presets, playlists and settings may be included in your Google account backup, handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. The analysis cache and crash file are excluded. You can disable this in your device's backup settings.

Children

Geode contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no user-generated content from other people. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

All Geode data lives in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app, or using Android's Settings → Apps → Geode → Storage → Clear data, removes it. Exported videos are ordinary files in the folder you saved them to and are deleted like any other file.

Third-party components

Geode bundles open-source libraries (including libprojectM, AndroidX/Jetpack and JTransforms). None of them collect or transmit data in this app. The full notices are in the app under Settings → Export & About → Open source licenses.
